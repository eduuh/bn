Radius & elevation

One radius token drives three steps; two shadows separate "sits on the page" from "floats above it". A dark surface gets most of its depth from the border, not the shadow — which is why there are only two.

Radius

rounded-sm

calc(--radius - 4px)

rounded-md

calc(--radius - 2px) — buttons, inputs, tags

rounded-lg

--radius — every card surface

rounded-full

chips and pills only — the one shape that ignores the token

Elevation

none

Flush — nested panels, rows inside a card.

shadow-card

Every card. Barely visible by design — the border does the work.

shadow-pop

Dialogs, sheets, the ⌘K palette — anything that traps focus.
