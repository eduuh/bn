You are doing a cross-branch status review.

1. Call `bn_summary` to get open todos and blockers grouped per branch.
2. Call `bn_todo` if you need the flat list of every open todo.
3. Lead with anything blocked — blockers first, by branch.
4. Then give a short per-branch rundown of open todos, heaviest first.
5. Suggest where to focus next and flag any branch that looks stalled.

Keep it scannable: group by branch, one line per item.
