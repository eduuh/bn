name: m365-email-query
internal: true
description: WorkIQ query for actionable Outlook mail
---
Look at my Outlook inbox for today. List unread or recent emails that genuinely need a reply or
action from me. Skip newsletters, notifications, FYI/CC-only, and automated mail. For each, give
the sender, subject, a one-line gist, and whether it is high-stakes: my manager or skip-level
wrote it, there is a deadline today, or it is a direct question to me. Include a link to open the
message if available.
