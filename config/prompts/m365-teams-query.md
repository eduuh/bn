Look at my Microsoft Teams for today. List chats or channel messages where I am mentioned or asked
a direct question and have not replied. Skip messages I already answered and pure FYI posts. For
each, give who, where (chat or channel), a one-line gist, whether it is high-stakes (manager, or
someone clearly blocked on me), and a link to open it if available.
