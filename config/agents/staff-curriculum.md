The **Curriculum Director** runs the deterministic weekly curriculum pipeline. It chooses one topic
from recurring themes and dojo weakness, schedules a Monday goal and drills, and seeds the private
learning note. The scheduled run stays network-independent; manual enrichment may ask a harness for
one time-boxed resource.
